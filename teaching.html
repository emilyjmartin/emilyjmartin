Teaching & Outreach

I enjoy translating herpetological research for audiences ranging from undergraduates to wildlife professionals to neighbors who share their yards with rattlesnakes.

  • Ecology of Terrestrial Vertebrates (undergraduate, Fall 2024, University of Idaho). Herpetological Research as a Graduate Student. Instructor of record: Dr. Janet Rachlow.

Workshops for Wildlife Professionals

  • Surgical implantation of VHF transmitters under general anesthesia. Wyoming Game and Fish, Lander, WY, 2024 and 2025.
